Desktop view, toolbar hidden

userflow 5.3 and 6: the desktop appears sized to the window, with nothing on top of it. The toolbar is hidden and slides in at the top edge or with Ctrl+Alt+Shift. The first time, a dismissible hint explains how.

Move the pointer to the top edge, or press Ctrl+Alt+Shift, to show the toolbar.
Keyboard order: every key goes to the desktop while it has focus (capture all keys is on by default); the toolbar shortcut is the only one the app keeps. Escape is never intercepted.

Takeover notice Proposed

protocol 5.7 and ipc.md 18: one active client per desktop. The displaced client is told another device took over, shows the other device’s name, and offers Revoke that device; it must not reconnect by itself. userflow does not describe this (open question).

This desktop was opened on another device

Maya’s laptop connected to Workstation and took over, so this session ended. Nothing on the desktop was closed.

Was that you?If not, someone may have your device key and PIN. Revoke that device, then change your PIN under Server details, Security.
Back to Home
Keyboard order: focus lands on Back to Home (the neutral action). "Revoke that device" signs in again with the PIN without taking the desktop, then confirms. "Take it back" reconnects and in turn displaces the other device; the app says so in the PIN prompt.
Accessibility: the device name is untrusted text from the server (sanitised, at most 64 bytes) and is shown as plain text.

Open link? confirmation Proposed

Open this link on your computer?

The remote desktop asked this computer to open a link. Nothing opens until you say so.

Link
https://docs.example.test/guide/getting-started?ref=desktop
It opens in your default browser, outside hash-xcreen. Only continue if you expected it.
Keyboard order: focus lands on Cancel; Open link needs a deliberate Tab and Enter. There is no "always allow" option by design. Only http and https links are offered; anything else is refused without a prompt.
Accessibility: the link is plain text, never rendered as a clickable link inside the app.
