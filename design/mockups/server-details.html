Danger zone: uninstall needs SSH Proposed

Conflict: userflow 10 puts Uninstall in the app, but architecture 5.3 and 8.5 make uninstall a root-only action that the agent cannot perform over its own connection. The mockup assumes the app reaches the server over SSH again (same host-key check as setup). See open questions.
