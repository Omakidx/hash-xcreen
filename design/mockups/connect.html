PIN prompt

userflow 5.1 and 5.2: Connect on a server card asks for the PIN. The PIN is checked by the server (architecture 8.3), never by the app.

Wrong PIN, waiting before the next try

protocol: after the n-th consecutive failure the device waits min(2^(n-1), 30) seconds; an earlier attempt gets BACKOFF with retry_after_ms and is not counted. The task brief called this "backoff"; userflow 5 does not mention it (open question).
